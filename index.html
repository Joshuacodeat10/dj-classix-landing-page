CLASSIX

For more than half a decade, DJ Classix has been elevating both social and corporate events; weddings, birthday parties, concerts, get-togethers, corporate galas, conferences, and brand launches within and across Nigeria.

He is also the Official DJ for Energize Music, a top label supporting creatives and artists within and beyond the Christian space. One of his breakout projects, DJ Classix Rooftop, champions Afro-gospel music and its culture, uniting people to celebrate and connect.

When DJ Classix is on the wheels, it’s a Classix Experience, where the energy, atmosphere, and connection light up the room every single time.
